== pattern dp-2d
complexity: O(n³) time · O(n²) space
nudge: Choosing the first balloon to burst leaves neighbours that depend on the other side. Which balloon in a span can you price without knowing anything else about it?
-- step Pad the row
1 balloons = 1, then nums, then 1
  hint: How do you give every balloon a neighbour on both sides?
  why: A 1 at each end never changes a product, and it removes every edge case.
2 memo = empty map from (left, right) to best coins
  hint: What do you remember between calls?
  why: The best score for each open span, since many choices lead to the same span.
-- step Spans too short to burst
3 if right - left < 2: return 0
  hint: When does a span between two boundaries hold no balloon?
  why: When the boundaries are adjacent: nothing lies strictly between them.
-- step Choose the last balloon
4 if (left, right) not in memo:
  hint: When do you need to compute the span at all?
  why: Only the first time it is asked for; after that the memo answers.
5     memo[(left, right)] = max, over every last strictly between left and right, of:
  hint: What do you try for a span?
  why: Every balloon as the one burst last, keeping the best.
6         balloons[left] * balloons[last] * balloons[right]
  hint: What does bursting the last balloon pay?
  why: It is last, so its neighbours are the span's own boundaries, which are untouched by definition.
7         + best(left, last) + best(last, right)
  hint: What about the balloons on either side of it?
  why: With last still standing until the end, the two sides never touch and are solved independently.
8 return memo[(left, right)]
  hint: What does the call hand back?
  why: The stored best for this span.
-- step Answer for the whole row
9 return best(0, last index of balloons)
  hint: Which span is the question?
  why: The whole padded row, between the two added 1s.
