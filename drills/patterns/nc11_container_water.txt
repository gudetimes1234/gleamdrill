== pattern two-pointers
complexity: O(n) time · O(1) space
nudge: Start with the widest container. The water is capped by the shorter line, so which pointer is worth moving inward?
-- step Start at both ends
1 left, right = first index, last index
  hint: Where do the pointers start?
  why: At both ends, the widest container there is.
2 best = 0
  hint: What do you track?
  why: The largest area seen so far.
-- step Measure, then move the shorter line
3 while left < right:
  hint: When do you stop?
  why: When the pointers meet there is no container left.
4     area = (right - left) * min(height[left], height[right])
  hint: How much water does this pair hold?
  why: The width times the shorter of the two lines.
5     best = max(best, area)
  hint: What do you do with it?
  why: Keep it if it is the best so far.
6     if height[left] < height[right]: left += 1
  hint: Which line do you give up?
  why: The shorter one. Every narrower container with it is capped by it and narrower, so it can never do better.
7     else: right -= 1
  hint: And if the right line is the shorter, or they tie?
  why: Move the right pointer in instead.
-- step Return
8 return best
  hint: What is the answer?
  why: The largest area over every pair that could have won.
