== pattern dp-1d
complexity: O(k·E) time · O(V) space
nudge: One round of relaxing every flight is one more flight taken, so the stop limit is a round limit. How do you stop two flights sneaking into the same round?
-- step Start at the source
1 costs = {src: 0}
  hint: What do you know before any flight?
  why: Reaching the source costs nothing.
-- step One round per flight
2 repeat k + 1 times:
  hint: How many rounds?
  why: k stops means at most k + 1 flights, one per round.
3     previous = copy of costs
  hint: What do this round's flights read from?
  why: A snapshot of last round's costs. Reading the live table would let two flights chain within one round.
4     for each (origin, destination, price) in flights:
  hint: Which flights does a round try?
  why: All of them.
5         if origin in previous:
  hint: Which flights can be taken this round?
  why: Only those from a city reached in earlier rounds.
6             total = previous[origin] + price
  hint: What would this flight cost in all?
  why: The cost of reaching its origin plus its price.
7             if destination has no cost or total is cheaper: costs[destination] = total
  hint: When do you update?
  why: When this is the first route there or the cheapest so far.
-- step Answer
8 return costs[dst], or -1 if missing
  hint: What if dst was never reached?
  why: No route within the stop limit exists.
== pattern bfs
complexity: O(k·E) time · O(V+E) space
nudge: Expand level by level, where a level is one more flight. How do you keep the frontier from exploding?
-- step Build the flights
1 outgoing = empty map from city to list
  hint: What do you look up at each city?
  why: The flights leaving it.
2 for each (origin, destination, price): add (destination, price) to outgoing[origin]
  hint: How do you fill the map?
  why: One entry per flight under its origin.
-- step Expand one flight per level
3 best = {src: 0}
  hint: What do you remember for each city?
  why: The cheapest cost found to reach it so far.
4 frontier = [(src, 0)]
  hint: Where does the search start?
  why: At the source, having spent nothing.
5 repeat k + 1 times:
  hint: How many levels?
  why: k stops is at most k + 1 flights, one per level.
6     following = []
  hint: What do you build during a level?
  why: The next level's frontier.
7     for each (city, spent) in frontier:
  hint: Which routes do you extend?
  why: Each route in the current level.
8         for each (destination, price) leaving city:
  hint: Where can each route go?
  why: Along any flight out of its city.
9             total = spent + price
  hint: What does the extended route cost?
  why: What it had spent plus this flight.
10             if destination has no best or total is cheaper:
  hint: When is the extension worth keeping?
  why: Only when it reaches destination for less than any route so far; that is what stops the blow-up.
11                 best[destination] = total; add (destination, total) to following
  hint: What do you record?
  why: The new best, and the route itself for the next level.
12     frontier = following
  hint: What comes next?
  why: The routes one flight longer.
13     if frontier is empty: stop
  hint: When can you stop early?
  why: When no route improved anything.
-- step Answer
14 return best[dst], or -1 if missing
  hint: What if dst was never reached?
  why: No route within the stop limit exists.
