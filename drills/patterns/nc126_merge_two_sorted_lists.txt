== pattern linked-list-pointers
complexity: O(n+m) time · O(1) space
nudge: The smaller of the two heads is smaller than everything still to come. What placeholder lets you attach the very first node the same way as the rest?
-- step A dummy to attach to
1 dummy = new node
  hint: What removes the special case for the first node?
  why: A dummy head: there is always something to attach to.
2 tail = dummy
  hint: Where does the next node go?
  why: After tail, the end of the merged list so far.
-- step Splice the smaller head
3 while list1 and list2 are both not None:
  hint: How long do you compare?
  why: While both lists have nodes.
4     if list1.val <= list2.val:
  hint: Which head goes next?
  why: The smaller one; taking list1 on ties keeps the merge stable.
5         tail.next = list1; list1 = list1.next
  hint: How do you take list1's head?
  why: Link it after tail and advance list1.
6     else: tail.next = list2; list2 = list2.next
  hint: And otherwise?
  why: Take list2's head the same way.
7     tail = tail.next
  hint: What moves after splicing?
  why: tail, onto the node just attached.
-- step Attach the rest
8 tail.next = whichever list is not None
  hint: What about the leftovers?
  why: The remaining list is already sorted and linked; attach it whole.
9 return dummy.next
  hint: Where does the merged list start?
  why: After the dummy.
