== pattern fast-slow-pointers
complexity: O(n) time · O(1) space
nudge: Read the array as a linked list where index i points at nums[i]. Two indices point at the duplicate, so the list has a cycle; where does that cycle begin?
-- step Meet inside the loop
1 slow, fast = nums[0], nums[nums[0]]
  hint: Where do the walkers start?
  why: Slow one step from index 0, fast two steps.
2 while slow != fast:
  hint: How long do they run?
  why: Until they meet, which they must inside the cycle.
3     slow, fast = nums[slow], nums[nums[fast]]
  hint: How does each move?
  why: One link and two links.
-- step Find the entrance
4 slow = 0
  hint: Where does slow restart?
  why: At the start; the start and the meeting point are the same distance from the cycle's entrance.
5 while slow != fast:
  hint: How long do they walk now?
  why: Until they agree.
6     slow, fast = nums[slow], nums[fast]
  hint: At what speed?
  why: One link each.
7 return slow
  hint: What is the duplicate?
  why: The entrance of the cycle: the value two indices point at.
== pattern binary-search
complexity: O(n log n) time · O(1) space
nudge: Search the values, not the positions. With no duplicate, exactly v numbers are at most v. What does it mean when more are?
-- step Search the value range
1 low, high = 1, len(nums) - 1
  hint: What values can the duplicate be?
  why: Anything from 1 to n.
2 while low < high:
  hint: When is the search done?
  why: When one value is left.
3     middle = (low + high) // 2
  hint: Which value do you test?
  why: The middle of the range.
-- step Count to decide which half
4     if count of numbers <= middle is more than middle:
  hint: How do you tell which half holds the repeat?
  why: Count the numbers at most middle; by pigeonhole, more than middle of them means a repeat among them.
5         high = middle
  hint: Then where is the duplicate?
  why: At or below middle.
6     else: low = middle + 1
  hint: Otherwise?
  why: Above middle.
7 return low
  hint: What is the answer?
  why: The one value left, the duplicate.
