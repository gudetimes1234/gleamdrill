== pattern binary-search
complexity: O(log n) time · O(1) space
nudge: The minimum is the one place the order breaks. Compare the middle with the right end: which half must hold the break?
-- step Bound the whole array
1 left, right = 0, last index
  hint: Where can the minimum be at first?
  why: Anywhere in the array.
-- step Halve towards the break
2 while left < right:
  hint: When do you stop?
  why: When the range is one element, and that element is the minimum.
3     mid = left + (right - left) // 2
  hint: Which element do you test?
  why: The middle of the range.
4     if nums[mid] > nums[right]: left = mid + 1
  hint: The middle is larger than the right end. Where is the break?
  why: To the right of mid: the order drops somewhere after it, and mid cannot be the minimum.
5     else: right = mid
  hint: And if the middle is smaller?
  why: From mid to right is sorted, so the minimum is mid or to its left. Keep mid in range.
-- step Return
6 return nums[left]
  hint: What is the answer once the bounds meet?
  why: The one element left is the minimum.
