== pattern bit-manipulation
complexity: O(1) time · O(1) space
nudge: XOR adds bits but forgets to carry, and AND finds exactly where a carry was owed. How do you combine the two until nothing is owed, and how do negatives survive in 32 bits?
-- step Fix both numbers to 32 bits
1 a = a AND MASK
  hint: What must you do to a before looping in an unbounded-integer language?
  why: Keep only its low 32 bits, so a negative number becomes its two's-complement pattern.
2 b = b AND MASK
  hint: And b?
  why: The same mask, so the carry loop works on 32-bit patterns and terminates.
-- step Add without carrying, then add the carries
3 while b is not zero:
  hint: When is the sum finished?
  why: When there is no carry left to add.
4     carry = ((a AND b) shifted left by 1) AND MASK
  hint: Where are carries owed, and where do they land?
  why: Wherever both bits are set; shifting left one moves each carry to the column it belongs in, and the mask drops anything past bit 31.
5     a = (a XOR b) AND MASK
  hint: What is the sum ignoring carries?
  why: XOR: one in exactly one input gives one, two ones give zero with the carry handled separately.
6     b = carry
  hint: What still has to be added?
  why: The carries, which become the next number to add.
-- step Read the sign back
7 return a if a <= LARGEST else NOT (a XOR MASK)
  hint: How do you turn a 32-bit pattern back into a signed number?
  why: A pattern above the signed maximum has its sign bit set; flipping the low 32 bits and then every bit gives the negative value.
