== pattern math
complexity: O(n²) time · O(n²) space
nudge: A quarter turn clockwise is two reflections you already know how to write. Which two, and in which order?
-- step Reflect through the main diagonal
1 columns = transpose of matrix (zip the rows together)
  hint: What is the first reflection?
  why: Through the main diagonal: zipping the rows makes each column into a row, which is the transpose.
-- step Reflect left to right
2 return each transposed row, reversed
  hint: What turns the transpose into a clockwise turn?
  why: Reversing each row mirrors it through the vertical centre line, and the two reflections together are a quarter turn clockwise.
