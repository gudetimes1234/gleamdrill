== pattern hash-map
complexity: O(log n) time · O(log n) space
nudge: Sums of squared digits are bounded, so the sequence must eventually repeat a value. What could you remember so that repeating is one lookup?
-- step Walk the sequence until it settles
1 seen = empty set
  hint: What do you remember as you go?
  why: Every value already visited, so a repeat is spotted in O(1).
2 while n != 1 and n not in seen:
  hint: When do you stop walking?
  why: At 1, which is happy, or at a value seen before, which means a cycle that will never reach 1.
3     add n to seen
  hint: What do you record before stepping?
  why: The current value, so arriving at it again is a detected loop.
4     n = squareDigits(n)
  hint: How do you take one step?
  why: Replace n with the sum of the squares of its digits.
5 return n == 1
  hint: What decides the answer?
  why: Whether the walk stopped at 1 rather than at a repeat.
-- step Sum the squared digits
6 total = 0
  hint: What does the helper build?
  why: The running sum of squared digits, starting empty.
7 while n is not zero:
  hint: When are the digits used up?
  why: When n reaches zero.
8     digit = n % 10
  hint: How do you get the bottom digit?
  why: The remainder after dividing by ten.
9     total += digit * digit
  hint: What does each digit add?
  why: Its square.
10     n //= 10
  hint: How do you drop that digit?
  why: Integer-divide by ten.
11 return total
  hint: What does the helper return?
  why: The next value in the sequence.
== pattern fast-slow-pointers
complexity: O(log n) time · O(1) space
nudge: The sequence ends in a cycle either way. Can two walkers at different speeds find that cycle without remembering anything, and how do you tell the happy cycle from the rest?
-- step Start the two walkers
1 slow = n
  hint: Where does the slow walker start?
  why: At n itself.
2 fast = squareDigits(n)
  hint: Where does the fast walker start?
  why: One step ahead, so the loop condition is not true before anyone moves.
-- step Run until they meet
3 while slow != fast:
  hint: When do you stop?
  why: When the walkers meet, which must happen inside the cycle the sequence falls into.
4     slow = squareDigits(slow)
  hint: How far does the slow walker step?
  why: One step.
5     fast = squareDigits(squareDigits(fast))
  hint: And the fast walker?
  why: Two steps, so it gains one step per round and must catch the slow walker inside the cycle.
6 return slow == 1
  hint: What does the meeting point tell you?
  why: If they meet at 1, the cycle is the fixed point 1 and n is happy; anywhere else it is not.
-- step Sum the squared digits
7 total = 0
  hint: What does the helper build?
  why: The running sum of squared digits, starting empty.
8 while n is not zero:
  hint: When are the digits used up?
  why: When n reaches zero.
9     digit = n % 10
  hint: How do you get the bottom digit?
  why: The remainder after dividing by ten.
10     total += digit * digit
  hint: What does each digit add?
  why: Its square.
11     n //= 10
  hint: How do you drop that digit?
  why: Integer-divide by ten.
12 return total
  hint: What does the helper return?
  why: The next value in the sequence.
