== pattern math
complexity: O(n) time · O(n) space
nudge: Adding one is a carry that starts at 1 at the last digit. Where does it stop, and what happens if no digit ever absorbs it?
-- step Start with a carry of one
1 out = []
  hint: What do you build?
  why: The result's digits, lowest first, reversed at the end.
2 carry = 1
  hint: What are you adding to the number?
  why: One, which is just a carry into the lowest digit.
-- step Ripple the carry upward
3 for digit in digits, from last to first:
  hint: Which end do you start from?
  why: The lowest digit, where the one is added.
4     total = digit + carry
  hint: What does this position hold now?
  why: Its digit plus whatever carry arrived.
5     append total % 10 to out
  hint: Which digit stays here?
  why: The last digit of the total.
6     carry = total // 10
  hint: What moves to the next position?
  why: The tens of the total, one only when the digit was 9.
-- step Grow a digit if needed
7 if carry: append carry to out
  hint: What if the carry survives every digit?
  why: The number was all nines and grows a new leading 1.
8 return out reversed
  hint: What order is out in?
  why: Lowest digit first, so reverse it to read most significant first.
