== pattern math
complexity: O(m·n) time · O(m+n) space
nudge: Digit i of one number times digit j of the other always lands at position i + j. What if you dropped every product into its slot and settled the carries only at the end?
-- step Read the digits lowest first
1 if either number is "0": return "0"
  hint: Which inputs short-circuit?
  why: Anything times zero is zero, and it saves stripping a string of zeros later.
2 a = digits of num1, lowest first
  hint: In what order do you want the digits?
  why: Lowest first, so a digit's index is its power of ten.
3 b = digits of num2, lowest first
  hint: And the other number?
  why: The same way, so positions add.
-- step Drop every product into its slot
4 slots = array of len(a) + len(b) zeros
  hint: How many digits can the product have?
  why: At most the two lengths added together.
5 for i, x in enumerate(a):
  hint: How do you pair up the digits?
  why: Every digit of a, with its position i.
6     for j, y in enumerate(b):
  hint: Against what?
  why: Every digit of b, with its position j, so each pair of digits meets once.
7         slots[i + j] += x * y
  hint: Where does this product land?
  why: At position i + j, since 10^i times 10^j is 10^(i+j). Carries wait until later.
-- step Settle the carries
8 carry = 0
  hint: What do you carry through the sweep?
  why: The overflow from the slot below, none at first.
9 digits = []
  hint: What do you collect?
  why: The final digits, lowest first.
10 for slot in slots:
  hint: In what order do you settle the slots?
  why: Lowest first, so each carry moves upward.
11     total = slot + carry
  hint: What does this position hold now?
  why: Its accumulated products plus the carry from below.
12     append total % 10 to digits
  hint: Which digit stays here?
  why: The last digit of the total.
13     carry = total // 10
  hint: What moves up?
  why: Everything above the last digit.
-- step Write the answer
14 return digits reversed as a string, leading zeros stripped (or "0")
  hint: How do you turn the digits back into the answer?
  why: Reverse to most significant first, join, and drop the leading zero an unused top slot leaves behind.
