== pattern dp-1d
complexity: O(n) time · O(1) space
nudge: The cheapest way to stand on a step is its own price plus the cheaper of the two steps below it. How far back do you ever need to look?
-- step Start for free
1 one_back, two_back = 0, 0
  hint: What does it cost to be on the ground before the first step?
  why: Nothing: you may start on step 0 or step 1, and you only pay a step's price when you stand on it.
-- step Carry the cheapest cost forward
2 for each price in cost:
  hint: How do you walk the steps?
  why: Once, front to back, so the two steps below are always already priced.
3     one_back, two_back = price + min(one_back, two_back), one_back
  hint: What does it cost to stand on this step, and what shifts down?
  why: This price plus the cheaper of the two below; the old one-back becomes the new two-back.
-- step Reach the top
4 return min(one_back, two_back)
  hint: The top has no price. Which steps can you reach it from?
  why: Either of the last two, so take the cheaper of the two final costs.
