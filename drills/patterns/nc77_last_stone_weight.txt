== pattern heap
complexity: O(n log n) time · O(n) space
nudge: Every round needs the two heaviest stones, over and over. Which structure gives up its maximum cheaply, and how do you get one from a min-heap?
-- step Build a max-heap
1 heap = every stone, negated
  hint: The heap module only gives a min-heap. How do you make it hand out the heaviest?
  why: Negate the weights, so the heaviest stone becomes the smallest value.
2 heapify(heap)
  hint: What makes the list a heap?
  why: Heapify arranges it in O(n), cheaper than pushing one at a time.
-- step Smash the two heaviest
3 while the heap holds more than one stone:
  hint: When does the smashing stop?
  why: When at most one stone is left.
4     heaviest = -pop(heap)
  hint: Which stone do you take first?
  why: The heaviest, negated back to its real weight.
5     following = -pop(heap)
  hint: And the second?
  why: The next heaviest, also negated back.
6     if heaviest != following: push -(heaviest - following)
  hint: What survives the smash?
  why: Equal stones destroy each other; otherwise the difference goes back in, negated again.
-- step What is left
7 return -heap[0] if the heap is not empty else 0
  hint: What if no stone remains?
  why: Answer 0; otherwise the last stone's weight, negated back.
