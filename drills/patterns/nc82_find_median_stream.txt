== pattern heap
complexity: O(log n) per operation · O(n) space
nudge: Split the values into a smaller half and a larger half; the median sits at the inner end of one or both. Which structure gives up its extreme value cheaply, and what rules keep the halves honest?
-- step Keep two halves
1 lower = empty max-heap (store negatives)
  hint: Where does the smaller half live?
  why: In a max-heap, so its largest value is on top; heapq is a min-heap, so store negatives.
2 upper = empty min-heap
  hint: And the larger half?
  why: In a min-heap, so its smallest value is on top.
-- step Add a number and rebalance
3 push value onto lower
  hint: Where does a new value go first?
  why: Into the smaller half, always; the next line corrects it if it belongs above.
4 move lower's largest onto upper
  hint: How do you keep everything in lower below everything in upper?
  why: Pass lower's top across. Whatever was largest in the smaller half now sits in the larger half.
5 if upper is bigger than lower:
  hint: Which size rule might that break?
  why: lower may hold one more than upper, never fewer.
6     move upper's smallest back onto lower
  hint: How do you restore the sizes?
  why: Return upper's top; it is the smallest of the larger half, so the order still holds.
-- step Read the median
7 if lower is empty: return 0.0
  hint: What if nothing has been added?
  why: There is no median, and the roots below would not exist.
8 if lower is bigger than upper:
  hint: How do you tell an odd count from an even one?
  why: Only an odd count leaves lower one larger.
9     return lower's largest, as a float
  hint: Where is the median then?
  why: The middle value is the top of the smaller half.
10 return the average of lower's largest and upper's smallest
  hint: And with an even count?
  why: The two middle values are the two roots; the median is their mean.
