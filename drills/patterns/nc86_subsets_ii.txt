== pattern backtracking
complexity: O(n·2ⁿ) time · O(n·2ⁿ) space
nudge: With equal values side by side, when you decide to leave a value out, how many of its copies should you leave out with it?
-- step Sort so copies sit together
1 return build(nums sorted)
  hint: What must be true of the input before the duplicate rule can work?
  why: Equal values must be adjacent, so all copies of one value can be skipped together.
-- step Stop at the empty list
2 if sorted_nums is empty: return [[]]
  hint: What are the subsets of nothing?
  why: Exactly one, the empty subset.
-- step Take the head, or skip every copy of it
3 first = sorted_nums[0]
  hint: Which value do you decide about?
  why: The head of the sorted list.
4 with_first = [first] + each subset of build(sorted_nums after the first)
  hint: What are the subsets that take the head?
  why: The head added to every subset of the rest; later copies may still be taken there.
5 past = 1
  hint: How do you find where the copies of the head end?
  why: Start a scan just after the head.
6 while past is in range and sorted_nums[past] == first:
  hint: When does the scan keep going?
  why: While it is still on a copy of the same value.
7     past += 1
  hint: Then?
  why: Step over that copy.
8 return with_first + build(sorted_nums from past)
  hint: What are the subsets that skip the head?
  why: Skip every copy at once. Skipping one and taking the next would rebuild a subset already found.
